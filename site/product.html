Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does Amber need my phone?

No. Amber carries its own eSIM connection and works on its own. The app is where conversations continue as text and where your vault lives. Useful, not required.

Can it hear the people around me?

Amber hears what you ask it to. It doesn't record rooms, meetings, or other people. Capture is always something you do on purpose, and whenever the microphones are open, the glass glows where everyone can see it.

How long does the battery last?

About two days of normal use. It charges overnight on its magnetic stand, and a 20-minute rest covers a long day.

Which languages does it speak?

More than forty, including live translation. Ask in yours, and it can answer someone else in theirs.

Is there a monthly fee or data plan?

No. The $249 covers Amber's built-in cellular connection and every feature, for the life of the device. No subscription, no carrier plan to sign up for, no surprises later.

Which phones does it work with?

Both. The companion app runs on iPhone (iOS 16 and later) and Android (12 and later). Amber also works on its own, so you can leave the phone behind entirely.

What if I change my mind?

Your reservation is fully refundable any time before Amber ships. After it arrives, you have 30 days to return it for a full refund, and every Amber comes with a two-year warranty.
