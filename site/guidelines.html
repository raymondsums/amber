Brand guidelines

The Amber brand system.

01 · LOGO

A name and a mark.

The wordmark is Amber set in Fraunces. The mark is the Orb: a glowing amber disc, the pendant seen from the front and the light itself. The word stays clean in menus and headlines; the Orb fills small square spaces like the app icon and favicon.

  • Always lit. The Orb carries its gradient everywhere. Never flat, never outlined.
  • Give it room. Clear space around the Orb equals its own radius.
  • One mark per view. It is the product's presence, not a repeated pattern.

02 · COLOUR

Two grounds, one light.

Warm and quiet on white, with a single dark "dusk" world for evening moments. Amber is the accent, and it appears only where Amber acts: its light, its words, its buttons. That one rule keeps the eye calm and lets the product feel present without shouting.

Contrast, measured (WCAG 2.1)

Colours were chosen for readability, not just warmth. Every text pairing was measured. One result became a rule: amber reaches only 2.2:1 on white, so amber never sits under text. It stays a glow, a mark, and decoration.

03 · TYPOGRAPHY

One workhorse, one signature.

Inter Tight carries everything: headlines tight at 620, body at 450, labels at 600. It is confident and easy to translate for a global audience. Fraunces, a warm serif, appears in one place only, the wordmark, so it stays a signature rather than a style. The warmth of the brand comes from the light, the colour, and the words, not the font.

04 · ACCESSIBILITY

Calm that works for everyone.

  • All text pairs meet WCAG 2.1 AA; core reading paths reach AAA.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible: a 2px ember outline, offset 2px.
  • Type scales fluidly, and body text never renders below 16px.
